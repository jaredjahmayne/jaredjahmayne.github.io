### Role Overview
As a freelance **Live and Broadcast Audio Engineer**, I bridge the gap between technical infrastructure and live performance. My focus is on designing reliable, low-latency signal paths and delivering high-fidelity mixes for both in house audiences and digital broadcast streams.

### System Design & Networked Audio
* **Audio Over IP (AoIP):** Design and deploy robust network topologies to route multi-channel audio efficiently across large-scale production environments.
* **Signal Flow & Interfacing:** Integrate physical stage inputs, including microphones and DI boxes, into digital networks through analog-to-digital converters and virtual soundcards.
* **Low-Latency Architecture:** Configure network switches and buffer architecture to ensure reliable, zero-dropout audio for live monitoring and broadcast.
* **System Calibration:** Utilize system measurement software and real-time DSP to align and tune PA systems, compensating for venue acoustics and correcting signals prior to the main mix bus.

### Broadcast Mixing & Live Sound
* **Digital Console & DAW Operation:** Manage complex bus routing, auxiliary effects sends, and master limiting in real-time across digital consoles and software mixers.
* **Gain Staging & Dynamics:** Implement precise gain structures and processing chains (EQ, dynamics, effects) to control unpredictable live dynamics and eliminate stage bleed.
* **Multi-Destination Mixing:** Deliver high-fidelity audio tailored for different mediums, simultaneously managing the acoustic energy in the venue (Front of House) and the translated mix for digital broadcast streams.
* **Live Troubleshooting:** Rapidly isolate and resolve signal path interruptions, feedback loops, and hardware faults without disrupting the live performance.

### Certifications & Credentials
* **Audio Networking Certification:** Level 1
